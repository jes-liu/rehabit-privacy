Rehabit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Rehabit ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide the app's functionality, including:

  • Account information: Username and authentication credentials.
  • Profile information: Habit names, descriptions, and preferences.
  • Timer information: Timer types, durations, and settings.
  • Counter information: Counter names and recorded counts.
  • Session information: Session dates, durations, labels, notes, and recorded counter values.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, synchronize profiles, and provide timer, counter, and session functionality.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising or behavioral profiling.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Rehabit does not intentionally collect analytics or tracking information. We do not use advertising trackers or track users across other applications or websites.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your information is stored in a PostgreSQL database and processed through our Django backend, hosted using Railway. These providers may process information as necessary to provide hosting and infrastructure services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including authenticated API requests, encrypted network connections, and secure authentication token storage. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account through the Settings section of Rehabit. Your account and associated personal data will be removed from our active application database, subject to data that must be retained for legal or legitimate operational purposes.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

7. Children's Privacy

Rehabit is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Rehabit evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
